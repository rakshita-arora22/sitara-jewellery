The Full Story

About

Sitara makes handmade jewellery inspired by India's craft traditions. Each piece is designed in small batches with skin-friendly metals, natural beads and hand-shaped terracotta. We work with local artisans and pack every order in recycled paper.

Mission

To make everyday style feel personal. We craft jewellery that is affordable, skin-friendly and made to be worn daily, and we pay our artisans fairly for every piece they finish.

Vision

To become the most loved handmade jewellery brand in India: one that keeps traditional crafts alive, chooses sustainable materials and makes every customer feel that a piece was made just for them.

How a piece is made

1

Sketch

2

Source

3

4
